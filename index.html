Swaddled Poster

A private tool for the Swaddled TikTok account (@swaddled.co).

Swaddled shares practical, save-worthy guides for new and expecting moms. Swaddled Poster prepares our photo carousels and sends them to the Swaddled TikTok account as drafts. The account owner reviews every draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to publish it.

The tool also reads the account's own post statistics (views, likes, comments, shares and follower count) so we can learn which posts are most helpful.

It is used only by the owner of the Swaddled account and is not offered to the public.
