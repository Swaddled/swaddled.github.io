Privacy Policy – Swaddled Poster

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Swaddled Poster is a private tool used only by the owner of the Swaddled TikTok account to prepare content and send it to that account as drafts.

What we access

When you connect your TikTok account, Swaddled Poster may access:

  • Basic profile information (such as your display name)
  • Permission to upload content to your TikTok drafts
  • Public statistics for your own posts (views, likes, comments, and shares) and account follower counts

How we use it

This information is used only to deliver drafts to your account and to review how your own posts perform so future content can improve. We do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

Storage

Post statistics are stored in a private project folder that only the account owner can access. Access keys are stored securely and are never published.

Removing access

You can disconnect Swaddled Poster at any time in your TikTok app settings under app permissions. On request, we will delete stored data.
